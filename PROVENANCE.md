# Provenance

`CoboundaryK3/Basic.lean` and the three files in `FalseControls/` were written for this
repository from *The permutation coboundary constant of the complete complex is k/3 for
4 ≤ k ≤ 8* (25 August 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22090958). The witnesses are those of
Appendix A, in gauged form, entered edge by edge.
