# Provenance

`CoboundaryK3/Basic.lean` and the first three files in `FalseControls/` were written for this
repository from *The permutation coboundary constant of the complete complex is k/3 for
4 ≤ k ≤ 8* (25 August 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22090958). The witnesses are those of
Appendix A, in gauged form, entered edge by edge.

`CoboundaryK3/AllK.lean` and `FalseControls/cross_degree_cheaper.lean` were added on
8 October 2026 for version 7 of the paper (*The permutation coboundary constant of the complete
complex is k/3 for every k ≥ 4*). The replicated `K₈` witness is the one printed in Appendix A of
version 7 (clusters `{0,1}, {2,3}, {4,5}, {6,7}`).

`paper/` holds version 7 of the paper, its LaTeX source and figures, and every script it runs,
with the output of the last run. `mixer/k3_mixer.html` is the interactive page made for the paper;
its numbers are computed in the page.
